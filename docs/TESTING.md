# Release validation

Bifrost releases use a compile-first validation policy. Real mod-stack compatibility is tested after a Workshop build is available because those combinations cannot be represented reliably before publication.

## Required before publishing

- Workbench script validation completes with zero Bifrost script errors.
- Game scripts contain no eager non-constant class-level static initializers; keep zero/null defaults and construct cached objects or collections on first use.
- The current compile log contains no candidate-caused VM, GUI, replication, or resource errors.
- Static integrity checks find no missing resource metadata, duplicate GUIDs, merge debris, empty files, or unresolved editor attribute classes.
- The release version, changelog, README, source fingerprint, candidate commit, and addon identity agree.
- `Scripts/WorkbenchGame/EnfusionMCP` is absent and no disposable agent-launched Workbench process is left running. Preserve any Workbench session the operator already had open.

## Multiplayer acceptance pass

Run this pass on a dedicated server with a remote Game Master, then reconnect that client for the JIP checks:

- Open CREATE in a fresh match and confirm every catalog folder starts closed. Confirm the five collapsed roots remain compact, contiguous 24-unit rows at the top of the browser instead of spreading vertically to fill the area above `Spawn vehicles crewed`. Expand beyond 22 rows and confirm Bifrost's scrollbar reaches the complete tree without resizing any visible row. Expand several folders, close/reopen Bifrost, and confirm the exact expansion state is retained. Start a new match and confirm the state resets closed. Select and place a vehicle from the Objects tab, return to All, expand Objects, and confirm `Vehicles` and its assets are still present in the regular folder tree. Confirm canonical APP-6 faction tabs remain evenly sized and retain their wide authored proportions. With only canonical factions loaded, confirm no puzzle control appears; load a mod with a non-canonical faction and confirm a puzzle appears directly beside Lightning, opens the third-party faction chooser, selects that faction, and remains highlighted while that faction is active. Under Lightning, confirm all Bifrost placeables are split into `FX` and `Bifrost`, and `Spawn vehicles crewed` sits immediately above `WORLD CAPACITY`.
- Enable `Spawn vehicles crewed` and confirm its bright checked `X` remains clearly visible against the accent border at normal and compact UI scales. Place representative driver-only, driver/gunner, and driver/gunner/commander vehicles. Confirm only operational seats receive riflemen, all spawned occupants are server-created and visible remotely, and the result remains correct after JIP.
- Open Vehicle Service and confirm the mode strip contains exactly `REPAIR` and `CARGO`, with no Armaments tab or ammunition quantity/type controls. First close it without running an operation and confirm the vehicle's normal door/seat actions remain available. Damage a vehicle, close Service, and immediately reopen it at least three times; confirm the render-only preview loads without an `Entity out of world bounds` assertion or crash. Then drain fuel and expend ammunition; run Repair, Refuel, Rearm, and Full Service separately. After each operation, close the menu, leave the service circle, and confirm the unchanged vehicle can still be opened, entered, driven, aimed, and fired. Rearm must restore each existing authored magazine/rocket barrel to its own authored capacity without changing the mounted weapon prefab. Place an unarmed fuelled vehicle and confirm Repair and Refuel remain available, Rearm is absent, and Full Service completes in the sum of only those two stages; repeat with repair-only, fuel-only, and no-service-capability prefabs. Repeat the entire operation matrix from a remote client and after JIP while checking the server log for a clean authoritative start/result and identical replicated vehicle state.
- In Arsenal, right-click Contents on an equipped vest and press Back; confirm Vests immediately repopulates the left selection panel without another click. Select Footwear once and confirm the left header and results immediately change to Footwear rather than remaining on Vests. Repeat with Escape and several other gear rows. For ALICE and Soviet harness variants, remove and add items through Contents and verify the displayed percentage and actual carried inventory change; repeat with a conventional vest as a control.
- In Gunsmith, click `WEAPON / SWITCH`, confirm the weapon carousel opens, change weapons, and verify attachments and staged preview follow the new selection.
- Place Loiter without editing its defaults and confirm it performs an armed gun run. Then exercise continuous fire and a configured aircraft/faction; verify pass-start and first-live-round diagnostics and confirm projectile load/spawn failures are explicit.
- Select multiple editable entities, open Compositions, enter name/category/author, capture, select the saved row, choose `PLACE IN WORLD`, and click terrain. Confirm relative transforms, hierarchy, scale, and terrain offsets are retained. Test invalid/missing content rollback, delete, per-GM undo, server restart persistence, remote placement, and JIP library synchronization.
- Open ordinary Properties on a vehicle and several other entities and confirm Mission Information never appears there. Open Scenario Settings, choose its dedicated `MISSION INFO` category, and edit Situation, Mission, Execution, Signal, and Intelligence. Confirm each value appears in the map journal for all clients, updates live through the server, survives remote-client reconnect/JIP, and does not grant edit authority to a non-GM.
- Under Lightning > FX, confirm the module is presented as `FX: Emitter`; place it, change each emitter preset/scale/enabled property, and confirm existing layouts containing the same prefab still load.
- With Nav Paths set to Selected and then All, issue a multi-leg route and confirm the whole current path, destination arrow, and final marker render rather than only an arrow over the unit. Reconnect the GM and repeat using the replicated fallback.
- Drag a placeable while the visibility overlay is enabled. Confirm the concealed/visible marker tracks the cursor smoothly every frame, never displays `Checking for player visibility`, and remains private to the GM interface. Kill a friendly unit as GM and confirm the vanilla perceived-faction notification is suppressed while Bifrost is open.

- Select an on-foot AI group and issue Wedge, Line, Column, and Staggered Column from Bifrost COMMAND. Confirm each formation is adopted while moving and while calm/idle, nested subgroup units and players are not repositioned, and the server logs `[DCO-GM] formation ordered` with at least one movement handler.
- Issue Stand, Crouch, and Prone from Bifrost COMMAND to one group and a multiselection. Confirm only direct AI members change stance, players remain untouched, remote clients see the result, and the server logs `[DCO-GM] server stance ordered` with the affected count.
- Place a paired Ambush Position and Ambush Kill-Zone, set matching Pair Tags, and send an AI group to the position. Confirm role colors, hold fire, kill-zone spring, explicit rearm, manual hold-fire independence, and the `[DCO-GM] ambush` state diagnostics. Repeat after the GM reconnects.
- Place a Defence Zone over an AI group and expose it to threats from two directions more than 20 degrees apart. Confirm it holds the defended area, reorients to the new contact rather than remaining latched to the first direction, reports contact loss, and does not take authority from a player-led group.
- Place a QRF Stage Area or QRF waypoint, direct an AI group to it, and create nearby same-faction contacts in distress. Confirm it deploys, retargets if a different group needs support, preserves and returns to its rally point on stand-down, and logs QRF responder, rally, deploy, and stand-down transitions.
- Place a CQB Clear waypoint on a building and direct a group to it. Confirm the remote GM sees a white building-bounds cue, the group enters and clears the structure, failure/completion diagnostics are meaningful, and the cue disappears when the intent ends. Repeat after JIP.
- Edit a waypoint's Completion Radius; confirm the white completion circle changes to the same distance, the server AI completes at that boundary, the `[DCO-GM] completion radius updated` line appears, and the value/circle survive JIP.

- Kill AI, destroy vehicles, and drop disposable inventory, then use `CLEAR GARBAGE`; confirm the server removes only dead AI bodies, destroyed unoccupied wrecks, and engine-tracked loose items. Living or incapacitated AI, player bodies, usable or player-occupied vehicles, non-deletable/blacklisted assets, and the current GM selection must remain untouched. Confirm every client observes the removals and the state remains correct after JIP.
- Select one group and then several groups, toggle `[ ] LOOP ORDERS`, and confirm the native cycle state repeats the placed waypoint chain, displays `[X]` on the remote GM, and remains correct after JIP.
- Configure a Loiter/Gun Run FX, enable continuous fire, close Properties, and confirm the deployed pass retains every fire setting on the server and remote client.
- Open Effects > Units without search and confirm `Animations FX` and `Arsenal Access` are adjacent and remain visible under every faction filter. Select Arsenal Access and confirm its icon and `TARGET` status remain contained inside the CREATE row at each supported UI scale. Confirm Arsenal Access appears as a script/system entry rather than an arsenal crate. Place it on a world prop and a vehicle; each must show `Open Bifrost Arsenal` above the selected surface, open the Bifrost arsenal from one F press, hide that world interaction prompt while the dialog is open, remain usable by remote players after the vehicle moves, reject duplicate placement, and preserve the interaction after JIP. Deleting the target must also remove its attached access script.
- As a non-GM remote client, verify Arsenal Access opens `BIFROST ARSENAL` on the GRS studio surface with `SOLDIER / GUNSMITH / KITS / SETTINGS` tabs, Bifrost accent colors, and the visible Bifrost footer icon. The live world and first-person weapon must remain outside the preview world, and the interaction prompt must disappear while the menu is open.
- In both player Arsenal Access and GM right-click `Edit Loadout`, overflow every Soldier list, the Gunsmith hardpoint rail, the horizontal candidate strip, and the Kits list. Confirm each list has a reachable scrollbar, wheel input moves only the list under the cursor, and the horizontal strip reaches both ends. LMB drag must rotate the operator or staged weapon, RMB drag must pan, and the wheel over open stage must zoom.
- Select each equipped primary, secondary, and launcher in Gunsmith. Confirm the camera glides to the weapon and selected hardpoint, only native-compatible attachments appear, changing an attachment updates the staged model and hardpoint label, and returning to Soldier restores full-character framing. Empty equipment slots must not flash or steal focus.
- On a dedicated server with one remote client and one JIP client, equip, clear, mount an attachment, wear the draft, enable and disable `APPLY CHANGES ON CLOSE`, save/load/rename/delete a kit, and exercise undo/redo. Confirm the server rejects an invalid target, oversized or incomplete kit stream, non-GM edits of another character, and remote applies outside an active Arsenal Access radius. Successful changes must replicate to every client and survive JIP.
- Confirm legacy GRS kit import is copy-only: imported kits appear in the shared Bifrost bank while the original GRS profile files or console rows remain intact.
- Click Animations FX and confirm its catalog row remains visibly armed with the accent arrow, accent label/icon, and `SELECT AI` status until an AI is selected or targeting is cancelled. Place it on an AI, verify every listed native animation can be selected and is visible on all clients, then enable `Leave when threatened` and drive the AI to alerted state.
- Ctrl-click a group without dragging and confirm native toggle selection still works; then Ctrl-drag it onto a trigger and verify the white line appears only after the drag threshold.
- Open a trigger and verify the larger Bifrost `SETUP / UNITS / FINALIZE` flow, conditional timer/action rows, and live Finalize summaries. Confirm every wrapped description expands its own option row without clipping or overlapping the next option. A close request from Setup or Units must route to Finalize; only `APPLY & CLOSE` may commit. Confirm a newly placed trigger is disarmed and the Stage Hidden warning is visible before applying.
- Configure staged spawn plus queued orders, activate/deactivate the trigger, and verify ellipse/rectangle areas, Present/Not Present, dynamic faction subjects, synced leader/any/all ownership, Countdown, Timeout, repeat cooldown, and Once state on the remote client and after JIP. Confirm dead units do not satisfy Present and a Once trigger remains active after its condition becomes false.
- Ctrl-drag AI groups to a trigger and exercise Linked Only, Stage Hidden, and Ambush Hold. Delete the unfired trigger after staging: every hidden group must return at its recorded transform, held groups must regain their prior fire state, and no pending trigger action may execute. For a repeat trigger, Ambush Hold must re-arm only after the activation condition clears.
- Open each custom Properties view and click the obscured world and menus; confirm the modal consumes those inputs, closes cleanly, and cannot leave an invisible input blocker behind. While trigger properties are open, floating utility panels must be hidden; after close/cancel/fallback, each must return to its exact prior visibility. Verify the trigger uses the Bifrost Properties layout rather than the legacy native form.
- Open the Forces Command picker at each supported UI scale, confirm the right-mouse-button glyph and `CHOOSE FACTION` stay inside the header, assign arbitrary available factions to all four watch slots, replace one slot, and confirm the selection remains stable for the session.
- Exercise the improved right-click Watch menus for units, groups, and triggers; confirm labels remain inside their panels at the supported resolutions and every long list remains scrollable.
- Open EDIT > PLAYERS as a remote Game Master, right-click a player row, and choose `Mark for Teleport`. Right-click empty ground and choose `Teleport Marked Unit Here`; confirm the server moves that exact player while preserving facing, every client observes the move, and the final transform remains correct after JIP. Confirm a limited or non-GM client cannot invoke the server mutation.

## After publishing

Test the changed features with representative third-party mods and confirm the combined Game module compiles without `Too many instructions per function`. Record reproducible failures for the next correction. Post-publication compatibility testing is not a blocker for publishing a compile-clean candidate.
